HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN IMAGES
==========================

Keep this "images" folder next to index.html.

1) LOGO
   Save your logo as:  images/logo.png
   (PNG with transparent background works best, ~200x200 px)

2) PORTFOLIO (Selected Projects)
   Put your project photos here:
     images/portfolio/branding-1.jpg
     images/portfolio/branding-2.jpg
     images/portfolio/branding-3.jpg
     images/portfolio/website-1.jpg
     images/portfolio/website-2.jpg
     images/portfolio/social-1.jpg
     images/portfolio/social-2.jpg
     images/portfolio/other-1.jpg

   Recommended size: about 1200 x 800 px, JPG or PNG.
   To use different file names, open index.html and change the src="..." paths.

3) TEAM PHOTOS
   Put square photos here:
     images/team/ssserwanja.jpg
     images/team/web-developer.jpg
     images/team/social-media.jpg
     images/team/bugembe-samson.jpg

   If a photo is missing, the site shows initials instead.

4) OPEN THE SITE
   Open index.html in your browser (same folder as images/).
   Or upload the whole folder to your web host.
